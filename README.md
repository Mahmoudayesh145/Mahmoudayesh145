## About Me

I am a motivated **Computer Engineering student at Cairo University** with a strong interest in artificial intelligence, full-stack development, and embedded systems.

I build practical and user-focused applications across web, desktop, mobile, and intelligent systems. My main areas of interest include **Agentic AI, Retrieval-Augmented Generation, Large Language Models, Computer Vision, backend systems, and embedded software**.

I enjoy learning how systems work from the hardware level to the application level and turning complex ideas into useful products.
